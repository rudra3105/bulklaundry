Our story

Quality on every order

Bulk Laundry was founded on a simple belief: every garment deserves professional care. We serve DFW families, professionals and South Asian communities with the same attention to detail on every single order.

Free pickup and delivery is our way of saving you time, so a clean closet doesn't cost you a Saturday.

Our process

Schedule

Book online, by phone or by WhatsApp for available pickup dates.

Collect

A driver picks up, tags and transports your garments.

Clean

Garments are inspected, stain-treated, cleaned and pressed.

Deliver

Returned pressed and packaged, with an SMS alert when the driver is on the way.
