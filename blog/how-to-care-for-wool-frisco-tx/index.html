How to Care for Wool Frisco TX

Cleaning, storing and preserving wool garments, sweaters, coats and blankets.

Cleaning, storing and preserving wool garments, sweaters, coats and blankets. This guide from the Bulk Laundry garment care team gives you the essentials, with practical advice you can use today.

Check the care label first

"Dry clean only" and "dry clean" labels mean the manufacturer recommends professional cleaning. When in doubt, a professional can inspect the fabric, construction and any stains and choose the safest method.

Practical tips

  • Treat stains quickly and tell your cleaner what caused them.
  • Don't iron over stains — heat can set them.
  • Store clean garments in breathable covers rather than plastic.
  • Empty pockets and close zippers before you hand garments over.

When to call a professional

Delicate fabrics, structured tailoring, leather, suede, and anything expensive or sentimental are best left to a professional. Our dry cleaning service includes free pickup and delivery on orders of $45+.
