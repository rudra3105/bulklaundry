Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about pickup and delivery, turnaround, specialty garments, memberships and pricing.

Pickup & Delivery

What is the $45 minimum?
Pickup and delivery are free on qualifying orders of $45 or more. Dry cleaning, wash & fold, sarees and household items all count toward the minimum.
Is a card required?
Yes. A valid card on file or prepayment is needed to schedule service. Payment is collected securely during booking.
How do I find my route day?
Pickups run Monday and Wednesday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Enter your ZIP code on our homepage, or start a booking, to see the route day for your address.
Do I need to be home for pickup?
No. Most pickups are contactless. Leave your bag in a secure, weather-protected spot and add access instructions when you book.
What should I put my clothes in?
Any secure bag works for your first order. After that we can provide a reusable garment bag. Clean orders come back professionally packaged.
What happens in bad weather?
If weather affects your route, we contact you and reschedule to the next available route day.

Turnaround & Speed

What is the turnaround time?
Monday pickups are returned Thursday and Wednesday pickups are returned Saturday. Rush service costs extra, so please call to confirm first.
Is same-day or express service available?
It may be available for some garments at select locations, depending on the garment, its condition, capacity and the daily cutoff time. Call 214-348-0666 to confirm.

Specialty Garments

Can I send sarees and wedding garments?
Yes. Sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, wedding gowns and other delicate or embellished pieces are accepted. Each one is inspected before cleaning.
Do you accept mail-in sarees from outside Texas?
Yes. Contact us first with photos so we can confirm your garment is suitable for mail-in, then we will send packing and shipping instructions.

Membership & Plans

How does the dry-cleaning membership work?
You pay $50 a month for $65 in eligible dry-cleaning credit, which is $15 of extra value every billing period. A three-month minimum applies and unused credit does not roll over. Available at select locations.
Do you offer recurring wash-and-fold plans?
Yes. Single is $49/month (40 lb), Couple is $99/month (80 lb plus 1 comforter) and Family is $199/month (180 lb plus 2 comforters). See the Terms page for details.
Do you serve businesses?
Yes. We offer recurring commercial laundry for linens, towels and uniforms. Visit our Commercial Laundry page to request a bid.

Pricing

How is pricing determined?
Starting prices are listed on our Pricing page. Final cost depends on garment type, fabric, construction, staining, embellishments, finishing and service speed, and is confirmed after inspection.
Can every stain be removed?
Stains are evaluated and treated case by case, but complete removal cannot be guaranteed.

Service Concerns

What if I have a concern with my order?
Contact us promptly. If you are not happy with a cleaning, the item receives one free re-clean, and concerns are reviewed under our Service-Care Policy. Results and embellishment stability cannot be guaranteed.

Still have a question? Contact us or call 214-348-0666.
