Frequently asked questions

What is the $45 minimum?
Pickup and delivery are free on qualifying orders of $45 or more. Dry cleaning, wash & fold, sarees and household items all count toward the minimum.
Is a card required?
Yes. A valid card on file or prepayment is needed to schedule service. Payment is collected securely during booking.
How do I find my route day?
Pickups run Monday and Wednesday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Enter your ZIP code on our homepage, or start a booking, to see the route day for your address.
Do I need to be home for pickup?
No. Most pickups are contactless. Leave your bag in a secure, weather-protected spot and add access instructions when you book.
What is the turnaround time?
Monday pickups are returned Thursday and Wednesday pickups are returned Saturday. Rush service costs extra, so please call to confirm first.
Is same-day or express service available?
It may be available for some garments at select locations, depending on the garment, its condition, capacity and the daily cutoff time. Call 214-348-0666 to confirm.
Can I send sarees and wedding garments?
Yes. Sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, wedding gowns and other delicate or embellished pieces are accepted. Each one is inspected before cleaning.
How does the dry-cleaning membership work?
You pay $50 a month for $65 in eligible dry-cleaning credit, which is $15 of extra value every billing period. A three-month minimum applies and unused credit does not roll over. Available at select locations.
