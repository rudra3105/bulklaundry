Grand Prairie FAQs

Where is the Grand Prairie location?
We don't list a store in Grand Prairie, but we pick up and deliver there. Enter your ZIP at booking to confirm your route day.
Is pickup and delivery free in Grand Prairie?
Yes, on qualifying orders of $45 or more in participating areas. A valid card on file or prepayment is required.
What services do you offer?
Dry cleaning, wash & fold laundry, saree and wedding garment care, household items such as comforters and rugs, and same-day / express service at select locations.
What is the turnaround?
Monday pickups return Thursday; Wednesday pickups return Saturday. Rush service may be available at select locations for an extra charge.

Yes. The dry-cleaning membership is $50/month for $65 in credit (three-month minimum, no rollover), and wash & fold plans start at $49/month.
