Privacy Policy

How Bulk Laundry collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Our services & locations

Bulk Laundry offers dry cleaning, laundry, free pickup and delivery, saree and wedding garment care, and same-day service across much of the Dallas–Fort Worth area. Coverage details vary by location.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form submissions, including your name, contact details, preferences and message.
  • Pickup and delivery bookings, which run through a third-party scheduling partner (SMRT) with its own privacy practices.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type, collected with cookies and analytics tools if enabled on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries.
  • To send updates about your orders, including by WhatsApp or text.
  • To analyze traffic and improve the website and our services.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp & text messages

Messages cover pickups, orders, payments and service changes, and may include occasional offers for customers who opted in. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages. Some replies may be automated, and you can ask to speak with a person. We do not sell or share your phone number for others' marketing. Standard message and data rates may apply.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party providers for scheduling, messaging, maps, forms and analytics. Each provider follows its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies, where used, help us understand visitors and measure ad performance. You can disable cookies in your browser, which may affect some site features.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, but no system can be guaranteed fully secure.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Reply STOP to end WhatsApp messages.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact us

Questions? Email info@bulklaundry.com or call 214-348-0666.
