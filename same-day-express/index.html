Same-day & express FAQs

Is there an extra charge?
Yes, a premium surcharge applies for express processing. The amount depends on garment type and detailing and is confirmed at booking.
What is the cutoff time?
It varies by location. Call 214-348-0666 to confirm.
Are all fabrics eligible?
Most are. Extremely delicate or heavily stained items may need more time.
Is same-day available near me?
It may be available at select locations, including Dallas, Frisco and Plano. Check the locations page or call.
What is the drop-off time?
Cutoffs and capacity vary. Please call before booking.
Is there a 24-hour option?
Yes — four stores have 24/7 drop-off kiosks: Dallas, Bedford, Flower Mound and Bartonville.
