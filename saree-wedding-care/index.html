Saree & wedding care FAQs

Is it safe to clean a heavy lehenga?
Yes, with the right method. We inspect weight, embellishments and construction first and use supported cleaning to prevent tearing.
Can you remove haldi stains?
Haldi (turmeric) stains are treated case by case. Many lighten significantly, but complete removal cannot be guaranteed.
How much does saree cleaning cost?
Saree cleaning starts at $25. Heavy beaded sarees start at $40. Final price is confirmed after inspection.
Is there a pickup fee?
Pickup and delivery are free on qualifying orders of $45 or more.
Do I need to be home for pickup?
No. Leave your garments in an agreed spot and add instructions at booking.
How long does it take?
Standard pickup turnaround applies for local orders; mail-in orders take 7–10 business days after intake.
Is rush service available?
It may be available for some garments at select locations. Call 214-348-0666.
Can I mail sarees from outside Texas?
Yes. Contact us first with photos, and we will send packing and shipping instructions.
Do you clean wedding gowns and veils?
Yes. Gowns, veils and formalwear are inspected individually. Boxed preservation is available.
